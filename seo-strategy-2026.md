## Remaining SEO Priorities

### Priority 1 — Google (Do First)

**Google Business Profile**
If not already claimed and fully completed, this is the single highest-impact action available. A fully optimized Google Business Profile drives the local "map pack" results that appear above organic search results for queries like "estate sales Houston" or "estate sale companies near me."
- Claim or verify the listing at business.google.com
- Complete every field: business name, address (2415 Taft St), phone, hours, categories (Estate Liquidator, Art Gallery, Auction House), services, description
- Add photos: gallery exterior, interior, sample pieces — updated regularly
- Enable and respond to Google Reviews — this is a trust signal for high-net-worth clients
- Set up Google Posts to promote upcoming sales events

**Google Search Console**
- Submit the sitemap at search.google.com/search-console
- Monitor for crawl errors, coverage issues, and Core Web Vitals
- Identify which neighborhood and service queries are generating impressions vs. clicks

---

### Priority 2 — Bing & Microsoft Search

Bing serves a meaningful share of search traffic, particularly among older and more affluent demographics — exactly the audience most likely to be managing an estate. Bing also powers Microsoft Edge, Windows Search, and Cortana.

**Bing Webmaster Tools**
- Create an account at bing.com/webmasters
- Verify the site and submit the sitemap (https://reevesestates.com/sitemap.xml)
- Bing's crawler is slower and benefits significantly from explicit sitemap submission
- Monitor for crawl issues specific to Bing

**Bing Places for Business**
- The Bing equivalent of Google Business Profile
- Claim and complete the listing at bingplaces.com
- Bing Places data also flows into Cortana, Microsoft Edge suggestions, and some third-party directories
- Many businesses skip this step — it's a low-effort, meaningful gain

**Bing-specific considerations**
- Bing weighs exact-match keywords in page titles and headings more heavily than Google
- Bing responds well to social signals — activity on Facebook and LinkedIn can positively influence Bing rankings
- Bing gives more weight to older domains and established backlinks — the 1969 founding story is an asset

---

### Priority 3 — Apple Search & Siri

Apple has become a significant search channel through Spotlight Search (on-device), Siri, Maps, and Safari Suggestions. For a local business serving affluent Houston clients — many of whom are iPhone users — this is underserved and worth addressing.

**Apple Business Connect**
- Apple's equivalent of Google Business Profile, launched 2023
- Claim the listing at businessconnect.apple.com
- Controls how Reeves Estates appears in Apple Maps, Siri results, Spotlight Search, and Safari Suggestions
- Add photos, hours, contact info, and a description
- Apple Business Connect data also feeds into Yelp (which Apple Maps uses heavily)

**Yelp**
- Apple Maps surfaces Yelp reviews and data prominently
- Claim the Yelp listing and ensure it is complete and accurate
- Encourage satisfied clients to leave reviews — even a handful of reviews significantly improves visibility
- Yelp also appears in Siri results when users ask for local businesses by category

**Siri and Spotlight considerations**
- Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone) across all platforms is critical for Siri to surface the correct business
- Schema markup (already implemented in layout.tsx) helps Apple's crawlers understand the business
- Ensure the website loads quickly on mobile — Apple prioritizes fast mobile experiences

**Safari Content Blockers**
- Safari blocks many ad trackers by default. Paid social targeting on Safari may underperform. Focus on organic search and email for Safari/iOS audiences.

## Social Platforms — Recommendations

Social media does not directly affect organic search rankings, but it drives brand awareness, referral traffic, and the kind of trust signals that convert high-net-worth clients. The estate sale audience spans two distinct groups: (1) the families and executors making decisions, and (2) the collectors and buyers who attend sales. Different platforms reach each group.

### Strongly Recommended

**Instagram**
- The highest-impact platform for this business. Estate sales, antiques, fine art, and Montrose/Houston culture are all naturally visual.
- Post: gallery pieces, behind-the-scenes staging, neighborhood context, "recently sold" highlights
- Reels showing a piece with its history and what it sold for would perform well
- Also builds a following of collectors and buyers who become repeat customers
- Handle to claim: @reevesestates (verify availability)

**Facebook**
- The platform with the widest reach among the core demographic: families managing estates, executors, and older Houston residents
- Facebook Groups for Houston estate sales and antiques have active communities
- Facebook Events for gallery sales — free, with meaningful local reach
- Facebook Ads allow hyper-local targeting by zip code, age, and interest (antiques, fine art, estate planning) — cost-effective for this audience

**Pinterest**
- Highly underestimated for estate sales. Pinterest is the platform people use to research furniture, antiques, decor, and design — exactly what buyers are looking for
- Pin gallery pieces, curated estate sale highlights, and "how to recognize quality" content
- Pinterest content has an unusually long shelf life — a pin can drive traffic for years
- Strong overlap with interior designers who are a secondary buyer audience

**LinkedIn**
- Targets the B2B referral channel: estate attorneys, financial advisors, CPAs, and trust officers who refer estate clients
- Post professional content: process explanations, estate planning context, market observations
- Connect with Houston estate law firms and wealth management professionals
- Less volume than Instagram but higher-value referral potential

### Worth Considering

**YouTube**
- Video walkthroughs of the gallery, behind-the-scenes of an estate being staged, or educational "what to expect" content
- YouTube is the world's second-largest search engine — "estate sale Houston" video results appear in Google
- Lower priority to start; higher effort, but long-term value

**Houzz**
- Platform for interior designers, architects, and home enthusiasts — active buyers of the kind of pieces Reeves handles
- A free business profile connects Reeves with designers who buy regularly for clients
- Particularly relevant for mid-century furniture and decorative arts

### Lower Priority

**X (formerly Twitter)** — smaller audience for this use case; primarily useful for industry networking with galleries, auction houses, and art market professionals

**TikTok** — younger demographic; less aligned with the estate sale client base, though "estate finds" content occasionally goes viral and could reach heirs and younger family members researching options
